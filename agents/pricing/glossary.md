# Pricing glossary

Terms that mean something specific inside the Pricing area (`src/domain/pricing.ts` and `test/pricing.test.ts`), each with what it guarantees. Commits and PR numbers refer to this repository (github.com/mrpia/mega-dev-s04-rentaplace). Written 2026-11-19 from the code and tests on main (72a41c1) and `memories.md`; where the code used a word two ways, the owner chose the meaning in a conversation that day.

- **Quote**: only what `quote()` in bookings.ts returns, the price of a stay a guest can reserve now, after the home is bookable, the stay meets the minimum and isn't in the past, and the dates are available. It guarantees the checkout charge: `createBooking` prices the same home, dates and guests the same way, and a test checks that the total and host payout match. [374848f, 860dbd9, 56c59df, cc7c390; decided in a conversation, 2026-11-19]
- **Price**: the `Quote` object `priceStay` returns for any valid stay of 1 to 90 nights, past dates included, without checking the home, the minimum stay or availability. It guarantees only the arithmetic, which is why the seed, `rap doctor` and old short stays can use it. [f440195, 06242ae, b1a74f1; decided in a conversation, 2026-11-19]
- **Nightly price**: one night's own price before any holiday surcharge, a line of the price's `nightly` array: the weekend rate on a weekend night, otherwise the weeknight rate. Accommodation is the sum of the stay's nightly prices, and the checkout date is never one of them. [f440195, 56c59df, b2b4f67; decided in a conversation, 2026-11-19]
- **Weeknight rate**: the listing's `nightly_cents`, what a Sunday to Thursday night costs; search filters and sorts by it and pages show it as "/ night". It guarantees nothing about a stay's price, which can include weekend nights and surcharges. [f440195, 74c56e9; decided in a conversation, 2026-11-19]
- **Weekend night**: a night that starts on a Friday or Saturday by the UTC weekday of its date (`isWeekend`), charged at `weekend_cents`. No test pins which days count, so a change to it has to be checked by hand. [f440195, 74c56e9, cc7c390]
- **Holiday night**: a night that starts on one of the listing's holiday dates while the listing has a surcharge percentage; its surcharge is that percentage of its own nightly price, rounded per night. A holiday on the checkout date costs nothing, and the surcharge stays out of accommodation but inside the subtotal. [b2b4f67, PR #2]
- **Subtotal**: accommodation plus holiday surcharge plus cleaning, the one base for the 12% service fee, the 8% tax and the 3% platform fee, each rounded once. It guarantees that the total is the sum of the rounded parts (46803, not 46804, for three 11001-cent nights plus cleaning) and that every `…Cents` field is a whole number. [f440195, 06242ae, 56c59df]
- **Host payout** (`hostPayoutCents`): the subtotal less the 3% platform fee, so it includes cleaning and the holiday surcharge, saved on the reservation at checkout. It is the host's share only as priced; after a partial cancellation bookings.ts recomputes it with its own 3% from the kept accommodation and surcharge. [f440195, 06242ae, e61b46a, b2b4f67]
- **Minimum stay**: three nights (`MIN_STAY_NIGHTS`), checked by `validateBookableDates` only where a new stay starts (`quote`, `createBooking`, `parseSearch`, `stayParams`); despite its name, that function checks dates, not whether the home is bookable. `validateDates` and `priceStay` keep accepting 1 to 90 nights, so the seed's 2-night stays and the 1-night price in the test still work. [b1a74f1, PR #3; decided in a conversation, 2026-11-19]
- **Valid date**: a `YYYY-MM-DD` string that survives a round trip through a UTC date (`validDate`), so 2026-02-30 is invalid even though `Date.parse` accepts it. `calendar.ts` relies on the same check for its month parameter. [f440195, 06242ae, 374848f]
