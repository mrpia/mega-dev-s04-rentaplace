Commits and PR numbers refer to this repository (github.com/mrpia/mega-dev-s04-rentaplace). Seeded 2026-11-18. The owned files were created in f440195 (`pricing.ts`) and 06242ae (`pricing.test.ts`) and changed since only in b1a74f1 (PR #3) and b2b4f67 (PR #2), both merged 2026-11-19.

## Internal engineering

- Take the 12% service fee and the 8% tax from the subtotal, round each once, and make the total the sum of the rounded parts. Rounding the total on its own gives 46804 where the test pins 46803 for three 11001-cent nights plus cleaning. [f440195, 06242ae, 56c59df]
- Every quote field whose name ends in `Cents` must be a whole number: the pricing test checks all of them, so a new share or rate added without rounding fails it. [06242ae]
- Cleaning is charged once per stay and is part of the subtotal, so the service fee, the tax and the host share all include it. The Lakeside A-Frame stay of 9 to 12 November 2026 (three weeknights) must stay at a 39000-cent subtotal: decision 0002, the pricing test and the `rap doctor` check all pin it, and CI runs the test but not the doctor. [f440195, 06242ae, 56c59df, 860dbd9]
- Keep the round-trip comparison in `validDate`: the regex only checks the shape, `Date.parse` accepts 2026-02-30, and the test pins that date as invalid. `calendar.ts` also calls `validDate` to check its month parameter. [f440195, 06242ae, 374848f; `Date.parse` checked in a conversation, 2026-11-18]
- The three-night floor lives in `validateBookableDates` (`MIN_STAY_NIGHTS = 3`), not in `validateDates`, which still allows 1 to 90 nights. Only the entry points for new stays call the floor: `quote` and `createBooking` in bookings.ts, `parseSearch` in search.ts and `stayParams` in `src/stay.ts`. `priceStay` keeps calling `validateDates`, so a 1-night stay still prices (the pricing test pins it) and the seed's 2-night stays still load. Moving the floor into `validateDates` or `priceStay` breaks both. [2026-11-19; f440195, b1a74f1, PR #3]
- `priceStay` calls `validateDates` without `future`, so it still prices past stays, which the seed needs for its stays from June 2026 and its completed demo trip. Refusing a past check-in is the caller's job: `quote` and `createBooking` in bookings.ts and `blockDates` in calendar.ts pass `true`; search and the stay defaults in `src/stay.ts` don't. [f440195, 06242ae, 374848f]
- The past-date check compares with the business date from `today()`, which reads `RAP_TODAY` (default 2026-11-09), never the wall clock, and check-in on that date itself is allowed. The tests set the same date and book stays from 10 November 2026 on. [f440195, 56c59df, 860dbd9]
- `quote` and `createBooking` each call `priceStay`, and checkout must charge exactly the quoted total, so the price may depend only on the home, the dates and the guests. A test checks that the booking's total and host payout equal the quote's. [374848f, 860dbd9, 56c59df, cc7c390]
- The 3% platform fee is written twice: `priceStay` takes it from the whole subtotal, cleaning included (the pricing test pins the host share at 37833 for a 39003-cent subtotal), and `cancelBooking` in bookings.ts takes it again from what the host keeps after a partial refund, the accommodation and holiday surcharge without cleaning. Change the rate or the base in both places, and in the help page and payouts page that tell hosts "less a 3% platform fee". [f440195, 06242ae, e61b46a, 74c56e9, b2b4f67]
- A change to the weekend rate, cleaning or tax can break `bookings.test.ts` too: its full-week strict cancellation (25 November to 2 December 2026, cancelled on 2026-11-11 after the free cancellation period; fixture rates 11000 weeknight, 14000 weekend, 6000 cleaning) pins refund 51060 and host payout 40255. The platform fee in `priceStay` doesn't reach those two numbers, because `cancelBooking` works out the payout with its own 3%, and `cancellation.test.ts` uses a hand-written price and never calls `priceStay`. [ccdfb16, e61b46a, 860dbd9, b2b4f67]
- The receipt in `web/components/stay.tsx` shows weeknights × `weeknightCents` and weekend nights × `weekendCents`, the listing's two flat rates, not each night's price. If a night's price ever depends on its date, as holiday rates would, change the receipt too or its lines stop adding up to the accommodation. [f440195, 74c56e9]
- A holiday night's surcharge is the host's percentage of that night's own price, rounded per night; `priceStay` keeps it out of `accommodationCents` but inside the subtotal, so the service fee, the tax, the host share and the 3% all include it and the receipt shows it on a line of its own. [2026-11-19; b2b4f67, PR #2]

## Customer facing

- Weekend nights are Friday and Saturday nights, charged at the listing's weekend price, and `isWeekend` takes the UTC weekday of each night. No test pins which days count, so check a change to it by hand. [f440195, 74c56e9, cc7c390]
- Checkout is exclusive: a stay from the 10th to the 12th is two nights, and the checkout date is never charged. That is also what lets a guest arrive on the previous guest's checkout day. [f440195, 56c59df, cc7c390]
- `priceStay` refuses more guests than the home sleeps (a test pins 5 guests in a home for 4), but the number of guests never changes the price. [f440195, 06242ae]
- The 12% service fee and the 8% tax are also typed into the receipt labels and the glossary, which says the service fee is refunded only with a full refund. Nothing links that text to the code, so change it with the rates. [74c56e9, cc7c390, 374848f, b2b4f67]
- A reservation keeps the price saved when it was booked: cancellation and payouts read the saved amounts and never call `priceStay` again, so a host's price edit changes only new quotes. [56c59df, f440195, 7f5d288]
- Minimum stay is three nights, the same for every listing and date, and hosts can't set their own; a shorter stay gets "Choose a stay between 3 and 90 nights." Quotes, new reservations, search and the dates on home and checkout pages enforce it. Reservations made before it keep their dates and can still be read, cancelled and retried. The holiday rule hosts asked for (tracker card `holiday-stay-length`) is still on hold. [decided in a conversation, 2026-11-19; b1a74f1, PR #3; glossary definition in PR #4, not merged]
- Whether a home can be quoted at all is decided in bookings.ts, not in `priceStay`: paused and closed homes are refused in `quote` and `createBooking` before any price is computed. [decided in a conversation, 2026-11-18; PR #1, d74d179]
- Each host sets holiday dates, a surcharge of 1 to 50% and refund days of 1 to 60 per listing, all three or none. The night that starts on a holiday date carries the surcharge, so a holiday on the checkout date costs nothing. [2026-11-19; b2b4f67, PR #2]
- The holiday surcharge is saved on the reservation when it is made, so a host's later holiday edits change only new quotes. [2026-11-19; b2b4f67, PR #2]
- The service fee is refunded with a full refund, as the code already did; the glossary's "never refunded" is corrected to match. [2026-11-19; b2b4f67, PR #2]
